👉 **Live Website:** [https://benzjeremy.github.io/itsbenzo-tv/](https://benzjeremy.github.io/itsbenzo-tv/)

---

## ✨ Features

- ⚡ **Zero-Bloat Web Architecture:** Pure HTML5 & CSS3 — no PHP backend, zero data leaks, and blazing-fast loading speeds.
- 🎮 **Authentic Creator Branding:** High-resolution branding assets and cyber-gaming design in emerald green (`#10b981`) and cyan (`#00f0ff`).
- 🌌 **Featured Streamers Showcase:** Dedicated section for community creators (**Chrii**, **Dinli_TV**, **AliPacinoRP**) with direct Twitch channels.
- 📡 **Privacy-First Live Stream Player:** 2-click consent modal for Twitch embeds compliant with GDPR.
- 🗓️ **Stream Schedule & Events:** Overview of Minecraft, Roblox, and community streaming sessions.
- 🔗 **Link-Hub Matrix:** Aggregates Twitch, Discord, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, WhatsApp Channel, Element/Matrix Space, and SoundCloud music.
- ⌨️ **Chat Command Directory:** Interactive list of chat commands (`!dc`, `!links`, `!matrix`, `!soundcloud`, `!friends`, `!lurk`) with 1-click clipboard copy and toast feedback.
- ⚖️ **Legal Compliance:** In-app / internal legal disclosure (§ 5 DDG) and GDPR privacy policy.
